Install on this phone

Install once from the browser, then open it from your Android or iOS home screen as a standalone app. The app shell works offline after the first load.

  1. Android Chrome: tap Install app, or open browser menu → Add to Home screen.
  2. iPhone Safari: tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. After install: open Bookkeeping AI from your home screen, then connect services while online.
2

Connect Google Drive

Use your Google account for the statement folder. This MVP stores the account label and folder link locally, then your Make/Zapier automation watches that Drive folder.

Open Drive Google sign-in
3

Connect parser automation

Paste the webhook that receives uploaded PDFs, runs Parseur/Nanonets/OCR, and returns extracted rows for import.

4

Connect AI categorisation

Choose how your automation will call an LLM. Keep API keys in Make/Zapier or your backend; do not paste secrets into this local demo.
